What We Do

Every piece of our work connects back to one thing: giving everyone a voice through art.

Art Program

Weekly 1-on-1 and small group sessions for neurodivergent students at our FCSN partner location.

Community Shop

Our shop gives local artisans a platform they often don't have the time to build on their own, brings hope to artists facing illness through their craft, and gives our student volunteers a stage for their own handmade work.

Giving Back

We're finding ways to give back to hospitals and families facing financial hardship. Small acts, added up, to bring a little more warmth to the people who need it most.
