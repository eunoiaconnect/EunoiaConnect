Volunteer With Us

No experience needed, just interest and a caring heart. There's a place for anyone who wants to help, whether it's our weekly FCSN sessions, our shop, or behind the scenes.

  • FCSN Session Volunteer — sit in on our weekly art sessions and support students directly.
  • Shop Volunteer — help make, package, or promote what we sell.
  • Social Media & Marketing Manager — share our story and help grow our reach online.
  • Fundraising Manager — help plan and run fundraising for hospitals and families in need.
  • Outreach Manager — help us connect with new partners, schools, and volunteers.

You'll also grow together with the team (think painting rocks together and swapping ideas at group events) plus weekly meetings to stay in the loop. If you're interested in getting involved in any way, simply fill out the Volunteer Form below and we'll reach out to you.

What You'll Do

  • Sit with FCSN students during weekly sessions, encourage exploration, help with supplies, and follow the session lead.
  • Ready for a challenge? Apply to lead a session yourself! Design the demo, plan the steps, build the slides. It's one of the best ways to grow as a leader.
  • Join weekly, online, volunteer meetings to update on and discuss how sessions and events are going.
  • Pitch in on the shop or sell your own crafts, or help with fundraising events. Volunteering isn't limited to the art class.

Time Commitment

Weekly sessions, roughly 2 hours per week. Free free to come for one session just to try it out! If interested, email keweili1028@gmail.com

Who Can Join

Middle school and high school students and adults welcome. No prior experience with special needs required. We'll teach you everything.
