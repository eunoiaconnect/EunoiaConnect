The Art Program

Process over product. Connection over perfection.

Our weekly art sessions are designed with one guiding philosophy: the goal is never a finished, "perfect" painting. The goal is a student who tried and learned something new, felt safe doing it, and left with a little more confidence than they arrived with. Art is the medium; connection and expression is the outcome.

How We Design Our Sessions

We partner with FCSN for our weekly art classes, building every session around sensory-friendly spaces, individualized support, room to communicate, and emotional safety.

How Sessions Work

Sessions are held weekly at our FCSN partner location in California. Each session runs about an hour and includes both 1-on-1 support and small group activities, depending on each student's needs. Materials are provided. No experience or preparation is needed from students or families.

Who Leads Each Session

Each session is led by a volunteer who prepares all of the course materials themselves by designing the demo, breaking it down into managable steps, and building the in-class slides. It's one of the best ways to build leadership and teaching skills as a volunteer. At the start of each semester, volunteers who are willing can apply to lead a session (or more!).
